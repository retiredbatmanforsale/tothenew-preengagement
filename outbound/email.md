## Notes for the sales team

- **Cold vs warm.** If cold, lead with the "every services firm" line. If warm, swap the opening paragraph for "[Mutual] suggested we connect — same context, would love your read."
- **Personalize in 15 seconds.** Scan the prospect's LinkedIn for a recent post or talk on AI / developer productivity. Reference it in one line above the body if you find one. If nothing obvious, send as-is — manufactured personalization reads worse than none.
- **CTA discipline.** The "30-minute call, no pitch" framing converts noticeably better than "explore opportunities" in this market. Don't soften it to "quick chat" — VPs read "quick chat" as "you'll try to sell me something."
- **Follow-up cadence.** Day 4, send a 2-line bump ("circling back — still worth a look?"). Day 10, attach `proposal.pdf` with a one-line note. Don't push past that — services VPs have long memories for spam.
- **The TTN name as proof.** If your prospect knows ToTheNew (likely if they're at a peer firm in India / digital-services space), the name carries weight. If they don't, "a digital engineering firm in the same competitive set" is a fair substitution in subject line variant 3.
